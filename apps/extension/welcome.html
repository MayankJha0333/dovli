Dovli is in your browser

Two quick steps, and anything you read can go straight into your notes.

  1. 1

    Pin Dovli to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle piece at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Dovli. Its icon stays in the bar, one click away.

    Dovli

    Not pinned yet

  2. 2

    Connect your Dovli account

    Dovli opens in a new tab. Sign in if it asks, then click Connect. You can switch the connection off any time in Dovli → Settings → AI apps.

Three ways to use it

  • Click the icon to clip

    Save the article, the whole page, your selection or a bookmark, or have AI write notes. Pick where it goes, add tags and a remark, and highlight the parts that matter first.

  • Open the drawer to write beside a site

    A Dovli page next to what you're reading: write, edit, tick to-dos. Changes from your other devices show up on their own. Also on the icon's right-click menu.

  • Right-click to add or highlight

    Selected text, a picture or a link on any website: right-click it and choose Add to Dovli, or Highlight with Dovli.

Change the shortcuts at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
